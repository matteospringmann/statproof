Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy covers StatProof Studio (the "App"), a private tool used only by the creator of the StatProof TikTok account to follow the performance of their own videos.

Who uses the App

The App is not offered to the public. Its only user is the owner of the StatProof account, who signs in with their own TikTok account.

What data the App accesses

  • Basic profile information of the signed-in account (display name and an account identifier).
  • The list of videos published by that same account, with their public statistics: views, likes, comments and shares, title or description, duration and publication date.

The App does not access other users' accounts, private messages, contacts, location or payment information.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to show the account owner how their own videos perform. It is stored on the owner's personal computer and is never sold, rented or shared with third parties, and never used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

The data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions), which stops all further data collection.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the StatProof TikTok profile: @statproof.
